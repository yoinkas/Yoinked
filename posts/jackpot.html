Writeup overview. I completed Jackpot as a TryHackMe Medium Linux target. The path was to enumerate every exposed service, understand the custom banking protocol, reproduce the activation-code logic from the supplied client, disable the fraud monitor, and drain the simulated cash cassettes to release the flag.

Reading the returned source showed a bank terminal status page, a downloadable /jackpot service tool, and JavaScript polling a status endpoint. Those clues connected the website to the terminal protocol and supplied the client binary needed for further analysis.

Exploit and completion explained. I wrote a Python client that followed the advertised frame format. It requested a fresh nonce, reproduced the FNV-1a-style activation algorithm used by the Jackpot binary, armed supervisor mode with command 0x04f0, disabled monitoring with 0x04f1, and repeatedly issued dispense requests until all 280 notes were removed. Once flag_released became true, command 0x04ff returned the flag. This completed the box by chaining the information learned from the web console, binary, and XFS gateway.
